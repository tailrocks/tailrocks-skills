# Etiquette and Hard Stops

## Hard stops

Stop the proposal on any row below:

- Assistance ban or a disclosure condition that the proposed work
  cannot satisfy.
- Claimed or assigned work, reserved newcomer work, or a current equivalent
  attempt without a materially different evidenced direction.
- Security-shaped evidence outside the declared private channel.
- A governance or proposal gate not yet passed.
- An unverified or false claim, a user unable to explain every
  line, or a request to hide assistance.
- Mass submissions, reward farming, or spam.
- Harassment or public protest.
- A pacing-limit breach.
- A dead or overwhelmed project where another contribution adds review
  burden.

On a stop, write the reason, evidence identity, date, and concrete
unclaimed or non-code alternatives. Never emit only "do not proceed".

## Proposal discipline

Use the required venue of the project and the exact disclosure
wording. When policy is silent, draft plain accountable disclosure. A
trailer is not disclosure.

Draft human communication for review. Never post it. Never write false
approval. Never claim work.

Keep one contribution in flight per project. Await its terminal state
or the evidenced patience window of the project. After rejection, ask
at most one genuine clarifying question. Then withdraw without public protest.

Minimize maintainer review burden in every choice. Preserve
correctness. Convenience of the contributor grants no exception.
