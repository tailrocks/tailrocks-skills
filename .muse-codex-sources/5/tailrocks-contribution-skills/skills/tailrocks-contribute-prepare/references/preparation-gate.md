# Preparation Gate

Verify every row below with current session evidence:

1. **Anchored:** accepted or linked issue, or a policy-permitted
   direct change.
2. **Scoped:** one logical authorized claim with no drive-by cleanup and
   no new capability.
3. **Base and history:** current target base, correct branch, and
   policy-compliant local commits.
4. **Proven:** baseline and final focused and full checks with nonzero
   units.
5. **Styled and documented:** repository-owned format, lint,
   documentation, and changelog rules.
6. **Template complete:** every required field and checkbox answered
   from evidence, or what, why, and exact verification without a
   template.
7. **Disclosure:** required or voluntary accountable wording in the
   required location.
8. **Hygiene:** no handoff directories, agent metadata, unrelated
   formatting, false dependencies, false evidence, or
   policy-violating footers in the target diff.
9. **Ownership:** user confirmation of understanding and maintenance
   for every line.

A failed or unavailable row blocks the prepared outcome. Never
downgrade it to prose. Add a legal trailer only with explicit user
authorization for that legal act. A defect correction includes
regression evidence that fails without it.
