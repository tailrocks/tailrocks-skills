## 1. Navigational & Usage Overview

Letter **M** contains several of the most frequently used operational verbs and adjectives in technical writing:
- **`MAKE SURE (v)`**: The central verification verb across the entire STE specification (used instead of unapproved *check*, *verify*, *ensure*, *make certain*, *see that*).
- **`MUST (v)`**: The mandatory modal auxiliary used to express strict necessity and legal/safety mandates.
- **The `MAIN` and `MAINTAIN` Pitfalls**: Unapproved *main* must be replaced by **`PRIMARY`**, and *maintain* must be replaced by **`KEEP`** or restructured around **`MAINTENANCE (n)`**.
- **The `MAY` Prohibition**: Prohibited in procedural steps; use **`CAN`** for possibility or state the explicit condition.

## 3. High-Frequency Pitfalls & Key Rules for Letter M

### 3.1 The `MAKE SURE` Mandate
- In ASD-STE100, the verb phrase **`MAKE SURE`** is the universal standardized verification instruction.
- It replaces all the following unapproved verbs:
  - *verify* $\rightarrow$ `MAKE SURE`
  - *check* (as verb) $\rightarrow$ `MAKE SURE` or `DO A CHECK`
  - *ensure* $\rightarrow$ `MAKE SURE`
  - *assure* $\rightarrow$ `MAKE SURE`
  - *confirm* $\rightarrow$ `MAKE SURE`
  - *ascertain* $\rightarrow$ `MAKE SURE`
  - *make certain* $\rightarrow$ `MAKE SURE`

### 3.2 The `MAIN` vs `PRIMARY` Mandate
- The adjective `main` is completely unapproved in ASD-STE100.
- Writers must always replace it with **`PRIMARY`**:
  - Non-STE: *Open the main valve.*
  - STE: `OPEN THE PRIMARY VALVE.`
  - Non-STE: *Connect to the main busbar.*
  - STE: `CONNECT TO THE PRIMARY BUSBAR.`

### 3.3 The `MODIFY` vs `CHANGE` Mandate
- The verb `modify` is unapproved.
- Replace with **`CHANGE (v)`**:
  - Non-STE: *Modify the wire harness.*
  - STE: `CHANGE THE WIRE HARNESS.`
- *Note*: **`MODIFICATION (n)`** is permitted as a Technical Noun under Category 20/21 when designating an official engineering modification order or service bulletin.
