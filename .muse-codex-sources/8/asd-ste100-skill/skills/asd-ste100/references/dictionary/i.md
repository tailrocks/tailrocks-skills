## 1. Navigational & Usage Overview

The table below catalogs all approved keywords and high-frequency unapproved guide words beginning with the letter **I**. 

- **Approved Headwords**: Displayed in **UPPERCASE BOLD** (e.g., **`IDENTIFY (v)`**, **`INSTALL (v)`**). Use only with the specified part of speech and approved meaning.
- **Unapproved Guide Words**: Displayed in *`lowercase italics`* (e.g., *`indicate (v)`*, *`inspect (v)`*). Must be replaced with approved alternatives or restructured.
- **Parts of Speech**: `(n)` noun, `(v)` verb, `(adj)` adjective, `(adv)` adverb, `(prep)` preposition, `(conj)` conjunction, `(pron)` pronoun, `(art)` article.
- **Help Classes**: Marked in the Help/Notes column as Help 1 (Operational/Collocations), Help 2 (Restricted meanings), Help 3 (Context-specific approvals), or Help 4 (Important notices/missing forms).

## 3. High-Frequency Pitfalls & Key Rules for Letter I

### 3.1 The `INSPECT` Prohibition
- In standard aviation and industrial documentation, `inspect` is one of the most common words.
- In ASD-STE100, **`inspect` is strictly unapproved**.
- **Correct replacements**:
  - For visual/physical examination: Use **`EXAMINE (v)`** (`EXAMINE THE BRAKE PULL CABLE FOR SIGNS OF WEAR.`).
  - For functional testing: Use **`DO A TEST`** or **`TEST (n)`** (`DO A TEST OF THE EMERGENCY LIGHTING SYSTEM.`).
  - For component measurements: Use **`MEASURE (v)`** (`MEASURE THE CLEARANCE WITH A FEELER GAUGE.`).

### 3.2 The `INSERT` vs `PUT` Mandate
- The verb `insert` is not approved in general technical actions.
- Writers must use **`PUT (v)`** or **`INSTALL (v)`**:
  - Non-STE: *Insert the pin into the slot.*
  - STE: `PUT THE PIN INTO THE SLOT.`
- *Note*: **`INSERT (n)`** is permitted as a Technical Noun (Category 1) when referring to a manufactured part (e.g., *threaded insert*, *helicoil insert*).

### 3.3 The `INDICATE` vs `SHOW` Mandate
- The verb `indicate` is unapproved.
- Replace with **`SHOW (v)`**:
  - Non-STE: *The red light indicates a low fuel condition.*
  - STE: `THE RED LIGHT SHOWS THAT THE FUEL LEVEL IS LOW.`
- Notice that **`INDICATION (n)`** is approved as a noun, but the verb must always be **`SHOW (v)`**.
