# ASD-STE100 Dictionary: Letter S

> **Governing Document**: ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, Issue 9 (2025-01-15)  
> **Source Section**: Part 2 – Dictionary, Physical Pages 367–398 (Printed Pages 2-1-S1 through 2-1-S32)  
> **Scope**: Compact table reference for Letter S: 134 entries.

---

## 1. Navigational & Usage Overview

Letter **S** is the largest and most complex alphabetical section in the entire ASD-STE100 specification, comprising 32 physical pages.

Key regulatory principles and pitfalls in Letter S include:
- **`SWALLOW (v)` (Help Category 3)**: Approved strictly in health, toxicological, and safety emergency contexts (e.g., warnings against swallowing toxic fluids). Prohibited for foreign object ingestion by machinery.
- **The `SHUT DOWN (v)` Phrasal Exception**: A fully approved phrasal verb used for deactivating engines, power plants, and computer systems.
- **Modal Verb Bans**: `shall` and `should` are strictly unapproved. Writers must use **`MUST (v)`** for mandatory actions or **`WE RECOMMEND`** for advisories.
- **The `SEARCH`, `SCRAP`, and `SECURE` Prohibitions**: Prohibited non-STE verbs replaced by **`LOOK FOR`**, **`DISCARD`**, and **`ATTACH / LOCK / TIGHTEN`**.

## 3. High-Frequency Pitfalls & Key Rules for Letter S

### 3.1 The `SWALLOW (v)` Context Constraint (Help Category 3)
- `SWALLOW` is an approved verb in ASD-STE100, but its usage is restricted exclusively to **Category 3 Help (Safety Instructions)**:
  - Compliant: `WARNING: DO NOT SWALLOW CLEANING SOLVENT. IF YOU SWALLOW THE CHEMICAL, GET IMMEDIATE MEDICAL AID.`
  - Prohibited: *The turbine engine swallowed a bird.* $\rightarrow$ `A BIRD WENT INTO THE TURBINE ENGINE.`

### 3.2 The `SHUT DOWN (v)` Phrasal Verb Exception
- While phrasal verbs are banned by Rule 9.3, **`SHUT DOWN`** is an authorized phrasal exception:
  - Compliant: `SHUT DOWN THE APU BEFORE REFUELING.`
  - Non-STE: *Turn off the engine.* $\rightarrow$ `SHUT DOWN THE ENGINE.`

### 3.3 The Modal Auxiliaries `SHALL` and `SHOULD`
- Both `shall` and `should` are prohibited in STE:
  - *shall* $\rightarrow$ **`MUST (v)`** for mandatory instructions or **`WILL (v)`** for declarative predictions.
  - *should* $\rightarrow$ **`MUST (v)`** if mandatory, or **`WE RECOMMEND THAT YOU...`** if advisory.
