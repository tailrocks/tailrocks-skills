> **Scope**: Compact table reference for Letter N: 32 entries.

## 1. Navigational & Usage Overview

Letter **N** covers essential negations, numerical references, and spatial/positional concepts:
- **Negation Discipline**: **`NOT (adv)`**, **`NO (adj)`**, and **`NONE (pron)`** are strictly regulated. Double negatives are prohibited, and conversational words like *never*, *neither*, and *nor* must be rephrased cleanly into standard active negations (`DO NOT... AT ANY TIME`, `NOT... AND NOT`).
- **The `NORMAL` vs `USUAL` Rule**: The ubiquitous English word *normal* is unapproved in STE. Authors must select **`USUAL`** or **`STANDARD`**.
- **The `NOTE` Rule (Rule 5.5)**: Procedural notes are introduced by the official label **NOTE**, but the word *note* cannot be used as an ordinary verb (*Note the reading* $\rightarrow$ `RECORD THE READING.`).

## 3. High-Frequency Pitfalls & Key Rules for Letter N

### 3.1 The `NORMAL` Prohibition
- In everyday technical writing, authors constantly refer to "normal operation", "normal pressure", or "return to normal".
- Under ASD-STE100, **`normal` is strictly unapproved**.
- **Approved Replacements**:
  - For standard operating parameters: Use **`USUAL`** (`THE SYSTEM PRESSURE RETURNS TO ITS USUAL LEVEL.`).
  - For established engineering baselines: Use **`STANDARD`** (`USE THE STANDARD PROCEDURE.`).

### 3.2 The `NOTE` Verb Functional Shift
- Writers frequently instruct technicians: *Note the oil pressure.*
- In STE:
  - **`NOTE`** is approved only as an editorial header for informational notes (Rule 5.5).
  - It is prohibited as an imperative verb.
  - Writers must state the exact physical task:
    - Non-STE: *Note the gauge reading.*
    - STE: `RECORD THE GAUGE READING.` or `LOOK AT THE GAUGE READING.`
