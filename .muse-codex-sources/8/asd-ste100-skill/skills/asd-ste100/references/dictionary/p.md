# ASD-STE100 Dictionary: Letter P

> **Governing Document**: ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, Issue 9 (2025-01-15)  
> **Source Section**: Part 2 – Dictionary, Physical Pages 329–346 (Printed Pages 2-1-P1 through 2-1-P18)  
> **Scope**: Compact table reference for Letter P: 130 entries.

---

## 1. Navigational & Usage Overview

Letter **P** contains key approved procedures, safety instructions, and major Issue 9 highlights:
- **`PREVIOUSLY (adv)` Approved in Issue 9**: One of the most important revisions in Issue 9. Previously unapproved in Issue 8, `PREVIOUSLY` is now an approved adverb meaning "at an earlier time / before" (e.g., `REMOVE THE SAFETY WIRE PREVIOUSLY INSTALLED.`).
- **`PUT ON (v)` Approved Phrasal Verb**: Phrasal verbs are generally prohibited by Rule 9.3, but **`PUT ON`** is explicitly approved for personal protective equipment and clothing (e.g., `PUT ON SAFETY GOGGLES AND RUBBER GLOVES.`).
- **The `PERFORM` Prohibition**: Prohibited across all STE writing. Authors must use **`DO (v)`** (e.g., `DO A TEST`, `DO MAINTENANCE`).
- **The `PROVIDE` and `PROPER` Bans**: High-frequency corporate jargon words like *provide* (`GIVE`, `SUPPLY`), *proper* (`CORRECT`), and *prior to* (`BEFORE`) are unapproved.

## 3. High-Frequency Pitfalls & Key Rules for Letter P

### 3.1 The `PREVIOUSLY (adv)` Revision in Issue 9
- In Issue 8 and earlier revisions of ASD-STE100, `previously` was non-STE. Authors were forced to rephrase sentences with *before* or construct awkward subordinate clauses.
- **In Issue 9 (2025-01-15)**, **`PREVIOUSLY (adv)` is fully approved**:
  - Compliant: `REMOVE THE GROUND LOCK PREVIOUSLY INSTALLED.`
  - Compliant: `CONNECT THE TWO WIRES PREVIOUSLY DISCONNECTED.`

### 3.2 The `PUT ON (v)` Phrasal Verb Exception
- Rule 9.3 explicitly dictates: "Do not use phrasal verbs."
- However, Part 2 contains a narrow set of authorized phrasal exceptions. **`PUT ON (v)`** is approved specifically for putting on safety clothing and protective gear:
  - Compliant: `PUT ON SAFETY GOGGLES.`
  - Prohibited: *Wear safety goggles.* (Note: `WEAR` is approved only for friction/erosion, never for clothes!).

### 3.3 The `PERFORM`, `PROVIDE`, and `PROPER` Prohibitions
- Three of the most common non-STE words in aerospace documents appear under letter P:
  1. *perform* $\rightarrow$ **`DO (v)`** (`DO A TEST`, `DO THIS PROCEDURE`)
  2. *provide* $\rightarrow$ **`GIVE (v)`** or **`SUPPLY (v)`** (`SUPPLY POWER`, `GIVE INSTRUCTIONS`)
  3. *proper / properly* $\rightarrow$ **`CORRECT / CORRECTLY`** (`CORRECT POSITION`, `INSTALLED CORRECTLY`)
