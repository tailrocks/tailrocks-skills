# ASD-STE100 Dictionary: Letters Q & R

> **Governing Document**: ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, Issue 9 (2025-01-15)  
> **Source Section**: Part 2 – Dictionary, Physical Pages 347–366 (Printed Pages 2-1-Q1 through 2-1-R18)  
> **Scope**: Compact table reference for Letters Q and R: 111 entries.

---

## 1. Navigational & Usage Overview

Letters **Q** and **R** govern mechanical rotation, repairs, references, and prefix disciplines:
- **The `ROTATE` vs `TURN` Prohibition**: One of the most widespread non-STE errors. The verb `rotate` is unapproved; authors must use **`TURN (v)`**.
- **Prefix `RE-` Discipline**: In standard English, writers freely coin prefixed verbs (*re-torque*, *re-install*, *re-clean*, *re-check*). Under ASD-STE100, if a `re-` verb is not explicitly listed in Part 2, it is prohibited: authors must use analytical constructions such as `AGAIN` (e.g., `TORQUE AGAIN`, `INSTALL AGAIN`, `DO A CHECK AGAIN`).
- **Core Verbs in R**: Includes essential operations like **`REMOVE (v)`**, **`REPLACE (v)`**, **`REPAIR (v)`**, and **`REFER (v)`**.

## 3. High-Frequency Pitfalls & Key Rules for Letters Q & R

### 3.1 The `ROTATE` vs `TURN` Mandate
- In standard technical writing, people constantly say *rotate the knob*, *rotate the shaft*, *rotate the engine*.
- In ASD-STE100, **`rotate` is completely prohibited**:
  - Non-STE: *Rotate the drive shaft clockwise.*
  - STE: `TURN THE DRIVE SHAFT CLOCKWISE.`
  - Non-STE: *Rotate the knob to position B.*
  - STE: `TURN THE KNOB TO POSITION B.`

### 3.2 The Strict `RE-` Prefix Prohibition
- Rule 1.4 dictates that writers cannot coin new verbs by attaching prefixes.
- Most English verbs starting with `re-` must be decomposed into the approved base verb followed by `AGAIN`:
  - *reinstall* $\rightarrow$ `INSTALL AGAIN`
  - *reconnect* $\rightarrow$ `CONNECT AGAIN`
  - *retighten / retorque* $\rightarrow$ `TIGHTEN AGAIN / TORQUE AGAIN`
  - *reuse* $\rightarrow$ `USE AGAIN`
  - *recheck* $\rightarrow$ `DO A CHECK AGAIN` or `MAKE SURE AGAIN`
  - *repeat* $\rightarrow$ `DO AGAIN`
- *Approved exceptions*: A small set of `re-` verbs have independent lexical status in Part 2, including **`RECEIVE`**, **`RECYCLE`**, **`REFUEL`**, **`REJECT`**, **`RELEASE`**, **`REMOVE`**, **`REPAIR`**, **`REPLACE`**, **`REPORT`**, and **`RETRACT`**.
