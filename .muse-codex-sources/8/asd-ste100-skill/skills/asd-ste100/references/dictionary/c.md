### <a id="can-v"></a><a id="can"></a>CAN (v)

- **Part of Speech**: `v`
- **Status**: Approved
- **Forms / Inflections**: `CAN, COULD No other verb forms.`
- **Approved Meaning**: Auxiliary modal verb that means to be possible, to be able to, or to be permitted to
- **Approved STE Examples**:
  - `A MIXTURE OF FUEL AND OXYGEN CAN CAUSE AN EXPLOSION.`
  - `YOU CAN CLEAN THE DRAIN HOLES WITH THE CLEANING TOOL.`
  - `YOU CAN OPERATE THE VEHICLE AFTER THE INSPECTION IS COMPLETED.`
- **Help / Usage Notes**:
  - *Do not use COULD (v) to show possibility*:
    - STE: `IF YOU DO NOT OBEY THIS WARNING, AN EXPLOSION CAN OCCUR.`
    - Non-STE: *If you do not obey this warning, an explosion could occur.*
