# ASD-STE100 Dictionary: Letters W through Z

> **Governing Document**: ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, Issue 9 (2025-01-15)  
> **Source Section**: Part 2 – Dictionary, Physical Pages 425–434 (Printed Pages 2-1-W1 through 2-1-Y2)  
> **Scope**: Compact table reference for Letters W, X, Y, and Z: 62 entries.

## 1. Navigational & Usage Overview

Letters **W** through **Z** conclude the ASD-STE100 vocabulary.
- **The `WEAR (v)` Dual Meaning & Clothing Restriction**: `WEAR` is approved **only** for erosion or damage caused by friction (`THE BEARING WILL WEAR`). It is strictly **unapproved** for clothing or protective gear (e.g., non-STE *wear safety goggles* must be written as **`PUT ON SAFETY GOGGLES`**).
- **The `WORK` Functional Shift Restriction**: **`WORK (n)`** is approved strictly as a noun. Using *work* as a verb is prohibited; authors must use **`OPERATE (v)`**.
- **Letter Z**: ASD-STE100 contains no entries under Letter **X**. The sole word beginning with **Z** is the unapproved verb *zero (v)* (indexed on physical page 433), which must be written as **`SET TO ZERO`**.

## 3. High-Frequency Pitfalls & Key Rules for Letters W through Z

### 3.1 The `WEAR (v)` Clothing vs Friction Rule
- One of the most famous traps in ASD-STE100:
  - **Permitted Sense (Friction/Erosion)**:
    - `THE TIRE TREAD WILL WEAR FASTER ON ROUGH RUNWAYS.`
    - `EXAMINE THE BRAKE PADS TO SEE HOW THEY WEAR.`
  - **Prohibited Sense (Clothing/PPE)**:
    - Non-STE: *Wear safety goggles.*
    - STE: `PUT ON SAFETY GOGGLES.` (Use approved phrasal verb `PUT ON`).

### 3.2 The `WORK` Functional Shift Restriction
- `WORK` is approved **only as a noun**:
  - Compliant: `COMPLETE THE ELECTRICAL WORK BEFORE FLIGHT.`
  - Non-STE: *The emergency pump works.*
  - STE: `THE EMERGENCY PUMP OPERATES.`

### 3.3 The General Recommendation GR-2 on `WITH`
- Preposition `WITH` is approved for instruments and tools:
  - `TIGHTEN THE BOLT WITH A TORQUE WRENCH.`
- However, GR-2 prohibits using `WITH` to connect simultaneous action clauses:
  - Non-STE: *Drain the oil with the engine hot.*
  - STE: `DRAIN THE OIL WHILE THE ENGINE IS STILL WARM.`
