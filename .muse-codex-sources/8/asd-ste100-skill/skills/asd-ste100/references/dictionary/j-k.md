## 1. Navigational & Usage Overview

Letters **J** and **K** represent relatively compact sections of the ASD-STE100 vocabulary. However, they contain high-risk false cognates and functional shift pitfalls (such as the prohibition against `jack` and `join` as verbs, the restricted safety role of `KILL`, and the specific approved senses of `KEEP` and `KNOW`).

## 3. High-Frequency Pitfalls & Key Rules for Letters J & K

### 3.1 The `KILL (v)` Scope Restriction (Help Category 3)
- `KILL` is an approved verb in ASD-STE100, but its semantic boundary is strictly circumscribed to biological destruction.
- **Permitted**:
  - `ADD BIOCIDE TO KILL BACTERIA IN THE FUEL TANK.`
- **Prohibited**:
  - *Kill the power.* $\rightarrow$ `DISCONNECT THE POWER.` or `SET THE POWER SWITCH TO OFF.`
  - *Kill the engine.* $\rightarrow$ `SHUT DOWN THE ENGINE.`

### 3.2 The `JOIN (v)` Prohibition
- The English verb `join` is unapproved because of its ambiguous mechanical, chemical, and metaphorical senses.
- Replace based on the exact physical mechanism:
  - Mechanical fastening: **`ATTACH (v)`** or **`CONNECT (v)`**
  - Chemical/adhesive: **`BOND (v)`**
  - Electrical circuitry: **`CONNECT (v)`**
