# Source Access for Strict ASD-STE100 Tasks

Use this reference when a Strict task needs a word, rule, or example
outside the bundled coverage. The bundled shards hold 1,956 entries.
They do not hold every Issue 9 headword. Absence from a shard is not
proof of unapproved status.

## Get the authorized copy

Obtain ASD-STE100 Issue 9 through an authorized channel. The official
source is `https://www.asd-ste100.org/`. Organizations in aerospace
and defense programs often hold distribution rights through ASD
membership or customer agreements. Confirm your own access before a
Strict audit. Never copy the official text into a public location.

## Consult the copy with host tools

Use the normal file tools of the installed agent. Open the PDF or
print copy at the needed Part and page. Read the dictionary entry
with its part of speech, approved meaning, forms, and Help notes.
Read the rule with its examples and Help boxes. Apply the finding to
the task. The authorized copy wins on every conflict with bundled
material.

## Record the result

Name the Issue 9 location behind each Strict verdict: Part, Section
or rule, and dictionary page or headword. When the authorized copy
is unavailable, record the check as blocked. Do not record a blocked
check as a pass.
