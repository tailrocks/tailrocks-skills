## 1. Master Rule-to-Reference Catalog

### Section 1: Words (Rules 1.1–1.14)
*Reference document:* [`words.md`](words.md) | *Technical categories:* [`terminology.md`](terminology.md)

| Rule # | Rule Title | Core Requirement | Reference Document |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Rule 1.1** | Approved Words | Choose words approved in the dictionary or authorized technical terms. | [`words.md`](words.md) |
| **Rule 1.2** | One Word, One Part of Speech | Use each word only as its approved part of speech; avoid functional shift. | [`words.md`](words.md) |
| **Rule 1.3** | One Word, One Meaning | Use words only with their approved meanings defined in the dictionary. | [`words.md`](words.md) |
| **Rule 1.4** | Permitted Forms | Use only permitted inflectional forms (verb tenses, adjective comparisons). | [`words.md`](words.md) |
| **Rule 1.5** | Technical Noun Categories | Use technical nouns in the 22 authorized domain categories. | [`terminology.md`](terminology.md) |
| **Rule 1.6** | Words in Technical Nouns | Words unapproved on their own may be used as part of a technical noun. | [`words.md`](words.md), [`terminology.md`](terminology.md) |
| **Rule 1.7** | Technical Nouns as Verbs | Do not turn technical nouns into verbs. | [`words.md`](words.md) |
| **Rule 1.8** | Company Glossaries | Keep domain-specific terminology standardized in project glossaries. | [`terminology.md`](terminology.md) |
| **Rule 1.9** | Short Technical Nouns | Use approved short forms or abbreviations when standard in the domain. | [`words.md`](words.md) |
| **Rule 1.10** | Jargon and Slang | Do not use informal jargon, colloquialisms, or non-standard slang. | [`words.md`](words.md) |
| **Rule 1.11** | Naming Consistency | Use the same term for the same concept; do not merge distinct items. | [`words.md`](words.md) |
| **Rule 1.12** | Technical Verb Categories | Use technical verbs only in the 4 authorized process categories. | [`terminology.md`](terminology.md) |
| **Rule 1.13** | Technical Verbs as Nouns | Do not turn technical verbs into nouns (nominalizations). | [`words.md`](words.md), [`terminology.md`](terminology.md) |
| **Rule 1.14** | American Spelling | Use American English spelling per Merriam-Webster's Collegiate Dictionary. | [`words.md`](words.md) |

### Section 3: Verbs (Rules 3.1–3.7)
*Reference document:* [`verbs.md`](verbs.md)

| Rule # | Rule Title | Core Requirement | Reference Document |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Rule 3.1** | Permitted Verb Forms | Use base verbs, simple present, simple past, past participle adjectives, imperative, and future (`will`). | [`verbs.md`](verbs.md) |
| **Rule 3.2** | Permitted Tenses | Use simple present, simple past, and simple future; avoid continuous and perfect tenses. | [`verbs.md`](verbs.md) |
| **Rule 3.3** | Past Participle Adjectives | Use past participles only as predicate adjectives after `be` or as noun modifiers. | [`verbs.md`](verbs.md) |
| **Rule 3.4** | Auxiliary Verbs | Use auxiliary verbs only as authorized (`be`, `have`, `do`, `will`, `can`, `must`). | [`verbs.md`](verbs.md) |
| **Rule 3.5** | "-ing" Forms | Do not use `-ing` words as verbs; use only if approved in dictionary or as technical modifier. | [`verbs.md`](verbs.md) |
| **Rule 3.6** | Active Voice | Use active voice; passive voice is permitted only in descriptions when the agent is unknown. | [`verbs.md`](verbs.md) |
| **Rule 3.7** | Verbs for Actions | Use verbs rather than nominalizations to express actions. | [`verbs.md`](verbs.md) |

### Section 8: Punctuation and Word Count (Rules 8.1–8.7)

| Rule # | Rule Title | Core Requirement | Reference Document |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Rule 8.1** | Semicolons Prohibited | Do not use semicolons in STE prose; split clauses into separate sentences. | [`punctuation-and-counting.md`](punctuation-and-counting.md) |

| **Rule 8.3** | Permitted Parentheses | Use parentheses only for the 7 authorized purposes (cross-refs, acronyms, part numbers, conversions, etc.). | [`punctuation-and-counting.md`](punctuation-and-counting.md) |
| **Rule 8.4** | Vertical List Word Count | Count the lead-in sentence and individual list items as separate word-count units. | [`punctuation-and-counting.md`](punctuation-and-counting.md) |

| **Rule 8.6** | Special Counting Groups | Apply the 7 special counting rules (numbers + units = 1 word; proper names = 1 word; identifiers = 1 word). | [`punctuation-and-counting.md`](punctuation-and-counting.md) |

### Section 9: Writing Practices & Recommendations (Rules 9.1–9.4, GR-1–GR-8)
*Reference documents:* [`writing-practices.md`](writing-practices.md) | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md)

| Rule # | Rule Title | Core Requirement | Reference Document |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Rule 9.1** | Sentence Reconstruction | Reconstruct sentences naturally to express meaning rather than performing word-for-word substitution. | [`writing-practices.md`](writing-practices.md) |
| **Rule 9.2** | Correct Word Choice | Use words in their authorized senses; do not misuse approved words in non-approved contexts. | [`writing-practices.md`](writing-practices.md) |
| **Rule 9.3** | Phrasal Verbs | Avoid phrasal verbs unless specifically listed as approved entries in the dictionary. | [`writing-practices.md`](writing-practices.md) |
| **Rule 9.4** | Style Consistency | Maintain consistent terminology and sentence structure across documents. | [`writing-practices.md`](writing-practices.md) |
| **GR-1** | Use of "that" | Use "that" to introduce restrictive relative clauses and clarify subordinate clause boundaries. | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md) |
| **GR-2** | Use of "with" | Use "with" only with a clear meaning (association, instrument, or accompaniment). | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md) |
| **GR-3** | Clear Pronouns | Ensure every pronoun has an unmistakable, unambiguous antecedent. | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md) |
| **GR-4** | Use of "this" | Follow "this" or "these" with a clarifying noun rather than using them alone. | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md) |
| **GR-5** | False Friends | Avoid false cognates across languages (e.g., actual vs. current). | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md) |
| **GR-6** | Latin Abbreviations | Replace Latin abbreviations (`e.g.`, `i.e.`, `etc.`) with explicit English words (`for example`, `such as`). | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md) |
| **GR-7** | Inclusive Language | Use gender-neutral, non-discriminatory technical terminology. | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md) |
| **GR-8** | Possessive Forms | Use apostrophe-s possessives for humans/teams; use prepositional "of" phrases for inanimate parts. | [`general-recommendations.md`](general-recommendations.md) |
