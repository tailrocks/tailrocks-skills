## 3. General Introduction Breakdown (Physical Pages 35–42)

- **Physical Page 35 (Page i):** Scope of STE as a controlled natural language for written technical documentation. Explicitly affirms STE is for *written* documentation, not oral/spoken interaction.
- **Physical Page 36 (Page ii):** Terminology harmonization with ISO 704:2022 and ISO 1087:2019. Guidance on authoring tools/checkers: tools are assistive and do not replace editorial judgment.
- **Physical Page 37 (Page iii):** STEMG accredited training recommendation. Legal non-significance clause: STE examples do not establish contractual warranties or liabilities. Controlled distribution notice.
- **Physical Page 38 (Page iv):** Chronological history of releases (1986 AECMA guide through 2025 Issue 9 standard).
- **Physical Pages 39–40 (Pages v–vi):** STEMG organization, national support teams (STEST), and official Change Form template.
- **Physical Page 41 (Page vii):** Dedications to past STEMG chairs; typographical conventions (bold uppercase for approved STE, regular/red for Non-STE).
- **Physical Page 42 (Page viii):** Normative bibliographical authorities (ANSI Z535, Merriam-Webster 11th ed., ISO 1087, ISO 3864, ISO 45001, ISO 704, Chicago Manual of Style 18th ed., Gregg, GPO).

## 5. Intellectual Property & Statutory Distribution Boundaries

Physical page 2 of Issue 9 establishes ASD's proprietary rights and identifies eight statutory categories entitled to free-of-charge use and reproduction:
1. National Associations who are members of ASD, and all their member companies.
2. Members of Aerospace Industries Association of America (AIA) and Aerospace Industries Association of Canada (AIAC).
3. Members of International Coordinating Council of Aerospace Industries Associations (ICCAIA).
4. Customers of companies in categories 1 through 3.
5. Ministries of Defense of member countries of ASD, AIA, and AIAC.
6. Airlines for America (A4A).
7. Airworthiness Authorities (e.g., FAA, EASA, UK CAA, Transport Canada).
8. Universities and research institutes for educational purposes.

To maintain strict respect for intellectual property rights, the `asd-ste100` skill is maintained in a **private repository** (`tailrocks/tailrocks-asd-ste100-skills`) with authenticated distribution.
