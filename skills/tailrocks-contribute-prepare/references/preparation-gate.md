# Preparation Gate

Every row needs current session evidence:

1. **Anchored:** accepted/linked issue or policy-permitted direct change.
2. **Scoped:** one logical approved claim; no drive-by cleanup or new capability.
3. **Base/history:** current target base, correct branch, policy-compliant local
   commits, and zero legal trailers or agreement actions.
4. **Proven:** baseline and final focused/full checks execute nonzero units; a
   defect correction includes regression evidence that fails without it.
5. **Styled/documented:** repository-owned format/lint/docs/changelog rules.
6. **Template complete:** every required field/checkbox answered from evidence;
   absent a template, state what, why, and exact verification.
7. **Disclosure:** required or voluntary accountable wording in the required
   location.
8. **Hygiene:** target diff excludes handoff directories, agent metadata,
   unrelated formatting, invented dependencies, fabricated evidence, and
   tool-default footers that violate policy.
9. **Ownership:** the user confirms they understand and will maintain every line.

A failed or unavailable row blocks `PREPARED`; never downgrade it to prose.
