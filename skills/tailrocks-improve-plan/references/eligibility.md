# Standalone-plan eligibility

A finding is eligible only when one fresh executor session can complete it,
confidence is HIGH, fix risk is LOW, no product or architecture decision remains,
and current evidence is sufficient. Effort may be S or M but never overrides
risk. LOW confidence produces an investigation plan. L scope, direction work,
security-boundary uncertainty, or any MEDIUM/HIGH fix risk routes to
`tailrocks-seed-roadmap`.
