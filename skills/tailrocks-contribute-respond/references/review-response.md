# Review Response and Follow-through

Fetch immutable review, check, comment, and head identities after exact approval.
Every new item is answered, fixed with evidence, or respectfully declined; draft
communication requires separate approval before posting.

Preserve the recon-recorded revision regime. Some projects review appended
fixups; others require a clean reroll with change notes. Recheck before the first
revision and never force-push without exact approval.

Treat a rerun command as a remote mutation. Request it only for an evidenced
transient failure under the project's policy. Do not silence or relabel real CI
failure.

Observe the recorded patience window, then at most one approved polite ping.
Never argue with rejection, relitigate a closed decision, target a maintainer,
or escalate publicly. An approved withdrawal states the truth briefly. After a
merge, record requested backports, release-note work, reverts, and regressions.

Deduplicate every remote action by object ID, head SHA, target, and body hash.
Uncertain success blocks retry until refreshed state proves what happened.
