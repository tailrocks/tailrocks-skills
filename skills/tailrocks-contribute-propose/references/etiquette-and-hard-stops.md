# Etiquette and Hard Stops

## Hard stops

- Assistance ban or disclosure condition the proposed work cannot satisfy.
- Claimed/assigned work, reserved newcomer work, or an existing equivalent
  attempt without a materially different evidenced direction.
- Security-shaped evidence outside the declared private channel.
- A governance/proposal gate not yet passed.
- Unverified/fabricated claim, user unable to explain every line, or requested
  concealment of assistance.
- Mass, reward-farming, spam, pacing-limit, harassment, or public-escalation
  pattern.
- Dead/overwhelmed project where another contribution would add review burden.

A stop writes its reason, evidence identity, date, and concrete unclaimed or
non-code alternatives. Never emit only “do not proceed.”

## Proposal discipline

Use the project's required venue and exact disclosure wording. When policy is
silent, draft plain accountable disclosure; a trailer is not disclosure. Draft
human communication for review—never post it, imitate approval, or claim work.

One contribution remains in flight per project. Await its terminal state or the
project's evidenced patience window. After rejection, ask at most one genuine
clarifying question, then withdraw without public escalation.

Every choice minimizes maintainer review burden while preserving correctness.
Convenience to the contributor grants no exception.
