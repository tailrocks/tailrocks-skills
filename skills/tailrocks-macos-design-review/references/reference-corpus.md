# Building taste that persists

Taste persists through annotated evidence, decisions, rejected alternatives,
and regression obligations. A folder of images is not a corpus.

## Positive references

For each admitted reference record:

```text
Application shape:
Screen and user job:
Rendered states and accessibility settings:

What works:
- information hierarchy
- native structure and behavior
- density, typography, motion, and content presentation
- keyboard and accessibility behavior

Material:
- functional regions using system material
- content regions deliberately remaining plain
- behavior against changing backdrops

Applicable mechanism:
Do not copy:
Evidence revision/date:
```

Use first-party platform applications with the same workflow shape, strong
platform-native products, direct competitors, and the product's own accepted
screens. Every entry needs alternate appearance, contrast, motion, keyboard,
and assistive-technology evidence applicable to its claim.

## Anti-references

Rejected work is often more discriminating than another adjective:

```text
Rejected state:
Observed failure:
Mechanism:
Correction:
Reusable rule:
Evidence revision/date:
```

Standing failure classes include glass as content decoration, action-capsule
noise, card grids without hierarchy, decorative backdrops added to showcase
material, custom blur, excessive borders, mobile navigation, hover-only action,
ambiguous icon-only controls, low-contrast text, fixed windows, avoidable modal
workflows, custom sidebars/tables weaker than native behavior, motion without a
reduced variant, placeholder-only review, and self-approval.

## Decision records

```text
Decision:
Reason:
Rejected alternatives:
Consequences:
Owner and date:
Evidence revision:
```

Record only genuinely contested choices. A decision without consequences is a
preference note, not reusable policy.

## Disposition loop

After an approved or rejected feature, disposition every learned item as one of:

- component-map entry;
- semantic token role with a committed value;
- positive reference;
- anti-reference;
- dated decision;
- rubric or anti-pattern rule;
- regression-preview or harness obligation;
- explicit rejection with reason.

No item may remain merely “learned.” Deduplicate by meaning, strengthen the
existing owner, and reject product-specific decoration that does not generalize.

## Restraint

More guidance does not monotonically improve output. Keep one owner per
responsibility, remove superseded entries, and prefer a narrow mechanism with a
known reader over a large archive. Platform defaults from another medium do not
transfer without a macOS-specific mechanism.
